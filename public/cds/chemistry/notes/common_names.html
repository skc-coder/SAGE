Sublimable Solids

These are solids that transition directly into a gas when heated. Exam questions almost always ask how to separate these from non-sublimable substances (like salt or sand). The answer is always sublimation.

SubstanceChemical FormulaKey Exam Context
Ammonium ChlorideNH4​ClFrequently paired with sodium chloride (NaCl) in questions. Heating leaves NaCl behind.
IodineI2​Sublimates to form distinct violet vapors. Often paired with sand or salt in separation questions.
NaphthaleneC10​H8​The material in mothballs; sublimates at room temperature over time.
CamphorC10​H16​OUsed in similar contexts to naphthalene for separation questions.
AnthraceneC14​H10​Another classic sublimable organic solid.
