1. Number Systems & Arithmetic Properties

1.1 Number Classification & Divisibility Rules

  • Divisibility Tests:
    • 2k: Last k digits divisible by 2k.
    • 3 or 9: Sum of all digits divisible by 3 or 9.
    • 11: Difference between sum of odd-position digits and even-position digits is 0 or divisible by 11.
  • Number of Factors: If N=p1a​p2b​p3c​, then: Total Factors d(N)=(a+1)(b+1)(c+1) Sum of Factors σ(N)=(p1​−1p1a+1​−1​)(p2​−1p2b+1​−1​)(p3​−1p3c+1​−1​) Product of Factors=N2d(N)​
  • Euler’s Totient Function ϕ(N): Count of coprime integers less than N: ϕ(N)=N(1−p1​1​)(1−p2​1​)…(1−pk​1​)
  • HCF & LCM Relation: For two numbers a and b: a×b=HCF(a,b)×LCM(a,b) HCF of Fractions=LCM of DenominatorsHCF of Numerators​,LCM of Fractions=HCF of DenominatorsLCM of Numerators​

1.2 Remainder & Recurring Decimals

  • Euler’s Theorem: If gcd(a,m)=1, then aϕ(m)≡1(modm).
  • Fermat’s Little Theorem: If p is prime and gcd(a,p)=1, then ap−1≡1(modp).
  • Wilson’s Theorem: For prime p, (p−1)!≡−1(modp).
  • Recurring Decimals:
    • Pure: 0.a1​a2​…an​​=99…9a1​a2​…an​​
    • Mixed: 0.b1​…bm​a1​…an​​=99…900…0(Full number)−(Non-repeating part)​

2.2 Quadratic Equations & Polynomials

For quadratic equation ax2+bx+c=0 (a=0):

  • Roots: x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​
  • Discriminant D: D=b2−4ac
    • D>0: Real & Distinct roots.
    • D=0: Real & Equal roots (x=−b/2a).
    • D<0: Complex conjugate roots.
  • Sum & Product of Roots: α+β=−ab​,αβ=ac​
  • Forming Equation: x2−(α+β)x+αβ=0.

3. Sequences, Series & Progressions

3.1 Arithmetic Progression (AP)

For AP with first term a and common difference d:

  • n-th term: Tn​=a+(n−1)d
  • Sum of n terms: Sn​=2n​[2a+(n−1)d]=2n​(a+l)
  • Arithmetic Mean (AM) of a and b: AM=2a+b​

3.2 Geometric Progression (GP)

For GP with first term a and common ratio r:

  • n-th term: Tn​=arn−1
  • Sum of n terms: Sn​=r−1a(rn−1)​(r=1)
  • Sum to infinity (∣r∣<1): S∞​=1−ra​
  • Geometric Mean (GM) of a and b: GM=ab​

3.3 Harmonic Progression (HP) & Means Invariant

  • Sequence a1​,a2​,… is in HP if a1​1​,a2​1​,… is in AP.
  • Harmonic Mean (HM) of a and b: HM=a+b2ab​
  • Inequality of Means: AM≥GM≥HM (Equality holds when a=b).
  • Relation: GM2=AM×HM.

4. Commercial Arithmetic & Quant

4.1 Percentage, Profit & Loss

  • Percentage Change: %Δ=OldNew−Old​×100
  • Profit & Loss: Profit P=SP−CP,Loss L=CP−SP Profit %=CPP​×100,Loss %=CPL​×100
  • Discount & Marked Price: Discount D=MP−SP,Discount %=MPD​×100
  • Single Equivalent Discount for d1​% and d2​%: deq​=d1​+d2​−100d1​d2​​

4.2 Interest & Installments

  • Simple Interest (SI): SI=100P×R×T​,A=P+SI=P(1+100RT​)
  • Compound Interest (CI): A=P(1+100R​)T,CI=A−P
    • Compounded half-yearly: R→R/2,T→2T.
    • Compounded quarterly: R→R/4,T→4T.
  • CI & SI Difference for 2 Years: D2​=CI2​−SI2​=P(100R​)2
  • CI & SI Difference for 3 Years: D3​=P(100R​)2(100300+R​)

4.3 Ratio, Proportion & Mixtures

  • Duplicate Ratio: a2:b2; Sub-duplicate Ratio: a​:b​.
  • Componendo & Dividendo: If ba​=dc​, then: a−ba+b​=c−dc+d​
  • Alligation Rule: If two ingredients of prices C (cheaper) and D (dearer) are mixed to get mean price M: Quantity of DearerQuantity of Cheaper​=M−CD−M​

4.4 Time, Work, Speed & Distance

  • Work & Efficiency: Work=Rate×Time. If A completes work in X days, 1 day work =1/X.
  • Speed, Distance & Time: Distance=Speed×Time.
    • 1 km/h=185​ m/s, 1 m/s=518​ km/h.
  • Average Speed: vavg​=Total TimeTotal Distance​
    • For equal distance at speeds v1​ and v2​: vavg​=v1​+v2​2v1​v2​​.
  • Relative Speed:
    • Same direction: vrel​=∣v1​−v2​∣.
    • Opposite direction: vrel​=v1​+v2​.
  • Boats & Streams:
    • Downstream speed u=vb​+vs​.
    • Upstream speed v=vb​−vs​.
    • Boat speed vb​=2u+v​, Stream speed vs​=2u−v​.

5. Geometry & 2D Mensuration

5.1 Triangles

  • Area Formulas: Area=21​×base×height=21​absinC Heron’s Formula: Area=s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)​where s=2a+b+c​
  • Equilateral Triangle (a): Height h=23​​a,Area=43​​a2,rin​=23​a​,Rcircum​=3​a​
  • Centres of Triangle:
    • Centroid (G): Intersection of medians. Divides median in 2:1.
    • Incenter (I): Intersection of angle bisectors. Inradius r=sArea​.
    • Circumcenter (O): Intersection of perpendicular bisectors. Circumradius R=4×Areaabc​.
    • Orthocenter (H): Intersection of altitudes.
    • Euler Line: H, G, O are collinear with HG:GO=2:1.

5.2 Quadrilaterals & Circles

  • Parallelogram: Area=base×height. Diagonals bisect each other.
  • Rhombus: Area=21​d1​d2​. Side a=21​d12​+d22​​. Diagonals bisect at 90∘.
  • Trapezium: Area=21​(a+b)×h.
  • Circle: Perimeter C=2πr,Area=πr2 Arc Length L=360∘θ​×2πr,Sector Area=360∘θ​×πr2=21​Lr
  • Circle Theorems:
    • Angle subtended at center is twice the angle subtended at circumference.
    • Angles in the same segment are equal. Angle in a semi-circle is 90∘.
    • Intersecting Chords Theorem: PA×PB=PC×PD.
    • Tangent-Secant Theorem: PT2=PA×PB.
