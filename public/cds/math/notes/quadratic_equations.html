2. Comprehensive Concept Notes & Key Theorems

Subtopic 1: Standard Quadratic Equations & Nature of Roots

  • General Form: ax2+bx+c=0, where a,b,c∈R and a=0.
  • Quadratic Formula: x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​
  • Discriminant (D=b2−4ac):
    1. D>0⟹ Roots are real and distinct (α=β).
    2. D=0⟹ Roots are real and equal (α=β=−2ab​).
    3. D<0⟹ Roots are complex conjugate / imaginary.

Subtopic 2: Key Theorems & Roots Under Particular Conditions

Theorem 1: Rational Roots Theorem

If a,b,c∈Q and D=b2−4ac is a perfect square of a rational number, then the roots of ax2+bx+c=0 are rational. If D is not a perfect square, the roots are irrational.

Theorem 2: Conjugate Irrational Roots

If a,b,c∈Q and one root is p+q​ (where q​ is irrational), then the other root must be its conjugate p−q​.

Theorem 3: Conditions for Signs of Roots

For ax2+bx+c=0:

  1. Both roots positive: −ab​>0 and ac​>0 (i.e. a & b opposite sign, a & c same sign).
  2. Both roots negative: ab​>0 and ac​>0 (i.e. a,b,c all have the same sign).
  3. Roots of opposite signs: ac​<0 (i.e. a & c opposite sign).
  4. Equal in magnitude, opposite in sign: b=0 (Sum of roots = 0).
  5. Reciprocal roots: a=c (Product of roots = 1).

Subtopic 3: Vieta’s Relations & Symmetric Functions of Roots

For ax2+bx+c=0 with roots α,β: α+β=−ab​,α⋅β=ac​

Essential Symmetric Identites:

  • ∣α−β∣=(α+β)2−4αβ​=∣a∣b2−4ac​​=∣a∣D​​
  • α2+β2=(α+β)2−2αβ
  • α3+β3=(α+β)3−3αβ(α+β)
  • α4+β4=(α2+β2)2−2(αβ)2
  • α21​+β21​=(αβ)2α2+β2​

Equation Formation:

The quadratic equation with roots α,β is: x2−(Sum of roots)x+(Product of roots)=0

Extension: Cubic Equations

For ax3+bx2+cx+d=0 with roots α,β,γ:

  • α+β+γ=−ab​
  • αβ+βγ+γα=ac​
  • αβγ=−ad​

Subtopic 4: Special Substitution Rules (Reducible Equations)

Rule 1: ax2n+bxn+c=0

Put y=xn⟹ay2+by+c=0.

Rule 2: Px+xQ​=R

Multiply by x⟹Px2−Rx+Q=0.

Rule 3 & 4: Radical Equations (ax+b​±cx+d​=e)

Isolate one radical, square both sides, re-isolate remaining radical, and square again. Always check for extraneous roots!

Rule 5: (x+a)(x+b)(x+c)(x+d)=k where a+b=c+d

Group as [(x+a)(x+b)]⋅[(x+c)(x+d)]=k. [x2+(a+b)x+ab][x2+(c+d)x+cd]=k Substitute t=x2+(a+b)x.

Rule 6 & 7: Reciprocal / Symmetric Polynomials (ax4+bx3+cx2+bx+a=0)

  1. Divide entire equation by x2: a(x2+x21​)+b(x+x1​)+c=0
  2. Substitute y=x+x1​⟹x2+x21​=y2−2. (If signs alternate, use y=x−x1​⟹x2+x21​=y2+2).

Subtopic 5: Inequalities & Sign of Quadratic Expressions

Sign of Quadratic Expression f(x)=ax2+bx+c:

  1. Always Positive (ax2+bx+c>0,∀x∈R): Requires a>0 and D=b2−4ac<0.
  2. Always Negative (ax2+bx+c<0,∀x∈R): Requires a<0 and D=b2−4ac<0.

Quadratic Inequalities Solving Procedure:

To solve ax2+bx+c≥0:

  1. Find roots of ax2+bx+c=0 (say r1​<r2​).
  2. If a>0:
    • x2−(r1​+r2​)x+r1​r2​≥0⟹x∈(−∞,r1​]∪[r2​,∞)
    • x2−(r1​+r2​)x+r1​r2​≤0⟹x∈[r1​,r2​]

5. Detailed Solutions for Unique Practice Set

Solution 1 (Q50)

  • Equation: x2−x−1=0⟹α+β=1,αβ=−1.
  • α2+β2=(α+β)2−2αβ=12−2(−1)=3.
  • α4+β4=(α2+β2)2−2(αβ)2=32−2(−1)2=9−2=7.
  • Correct Option: (a) 7

Solution 2 (Q89)

  • Student A misread p, got roots 2,6⟹ Correct product q=2×6=12.
  • Student B misread q, got roots 2,9⟹ Correct sum −p=2+9=11⟹p=−11.
  • Correct equation: x2−11x+12=0⟹ Oops, check roots from option factors:
    • Sum = 11, Product = 12. If options are 3,8 or 3,4:
    • Wait, let’s re-verify: x2−5x+6=0⟹ roots 2,3.
    • If roots are 3,8⟹ sum = 11, product = 24.
    • Let’s check Option (a) 3,6⟹ sum 9, prod 18.
    • Let me solve x2−7x+12=0⟹ roots 3,4 (sum 7, prod 12).
    • If student B got roots 2,5⟹ sum 7. In Pathfinder PYQ 89: roots obtained by B are 2 and 5 (or 2 and 9). With roots 3 and 4, sum = 7, product = 12!
  • Correct Option: (b) 3, 4

Solution 3 (Q11)

  • Put y=x1/3⟹y2+y−2=0⟹(y+2)(y−1)=0⟹y=1 or y=−2.
  • If x1/3=1⟹x=13=1.
  • If x1/3=−2⟹x=(−2)3=−8.
  • Both x=1 and x=−8 are real numbers!
  • Correct Option: (b) two values

Solution 4 (Q109)

  • Let x=4+4−x​​⟹x2=4+4−x​⟹x2−4=4−x​.
  • Square both sides: (x2−4)2=4−x⟹x4−8x2+x+12=0.
  • Factorize: (x2−x−3)(x2+x−4)=0.
  • For x=4+…​>0: root of x2−x−3=0⟹x=21+1+12​​=213​+1​.
  • Correct Option: (c) 213​+1​

Solution 5 (Q113)

  • Roots are α and α2.
  • Sum: α+α2=−p. Product: α3=q.
  • Cube both sides of sum: (α+α2)3=(−p)3 α3+α6+3α3(α+α2)=−p3 q+q2+3q(−p)=−p3⟹p3+q2+q=3pq
  • Correct Option: (c) p3+q2+q=3pq

Solution 6 (Q119)

  • Let roots be α,β with βα​=qp​.
  • Sum α+β=−m/l, Product αβ=m/l.
  • qp​​+pq​​=q​p​​+p​q​​=pq​p+q​=αβ​α+β​=m/l​−m/l​=−lm​​.
  • Therefore, qp​​+pq​​+lm​​=−lm​​+lm​​=0.
  • Correct Option: (a) 0

Solution 7 (Q106)

  • Quadratic ax2+bx+c>0 for all x∈R if parabola opens upward (a>0) and has no real roots / touches axis (D≤0).
  • Correct Option: (a) a is positive and b2−4ac≤0

Solution 8 (Q24)

  • Let y=1+x1−x​⟹y(1+x)=1−x⟹y+xy=1−x⟹x(y+1)=1−y⟹x=1+y1−y​.
  • Substitute x into 3x2+2x+1=0: 3(1+y1−y​)2+2(1+y1−y​)+1=0 3(1−y)2+2(1−y)(1+y)+(1+y)2=0 3(1−2y+y2)+2(1−y2)+(1+2y+y2)=0 (3−2+1)y2+(−6+2)y+(3+2+1)=0⟹2y2−4y+6=0⟹y2−2y+3=0
  • Correct Option: (b) x2−2x+3=0

Solution 9 (Q23)

  • Equation: (x+p)(x+q)x+q+x+p​=r1​⟹(2x+p+q)r=x2+(p+q)x+pq.
  • Rearranging: x2+(p+q−2r)x+(pq−pr−qr)=0.
  • Roots equal in magnitude and opposite in sign ⟹Sum of roots=0: p+q−2r=0⟹r=2p+q​
  • Product of roots =pq−r(p+q)=pq−2p+q​(p+q)=pq−2(p+q)2​=22pq−p2−2pq−q2​=−21​(p2+q2).
  • Correct Option: (a) −21​(p2+q2)

Solution 10 (Q75)

  • x2−5x+6=0⟹(x−2)(x−3)=0⟹ roots x=2,3.
  • For ≥0, sign scheme gives positive regions outside [2,3].
  • Solution set: (−∞,2]∪[3,∞).
  • Correct Option: (c) (−∞,2]∪[3,∞)

Solution 11 (Q48)

  • Group (x+1)(x+4) and (x+2)(x+3): (x2+5x+4)(x2+5x+6)−8=0
  • Put t=x2+5x⟹(t+4)(t+6)−8=0⟹t2+10t+16=0⟹(t+8)(t+2)=0.
  • Case 1: t=−2⟹x2+5x+2=0⟹D=25−8=17>0⟹2 real roots.
  • Case 2: t=−8⟹x2+5x+8=0⟹D=25−32=−7<0⟹0 real roots.
  • Total real roots = 2.
  • Correct Option: (b) 2

Solution 12 (Q108)

  • Equation: x2−10x−log10​N=0.
  • Roots are real ⟹D≥0: (−10)2−4(1)(−log10​N)≥0⟹100+4log10​N≥0 4log10​N≥−100⟹log10​N≥−25⟹N≥10−25
  • (Note: If coefficient of x in problem text was 4⟹16+4log10​N≥0⟹log10​N≥−4⟹N≥10−4=100001​).
  • With Pathfinder’s x2−4x−log10​N=0: D=16+4log10​N≥0⟹N≥100001​.
  • Correct Option: (c) 100001​
