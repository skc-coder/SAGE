## 1. Where Economics Sits in the CDS Exam

| Paper                  | Marks                  | Economics Weight                        |
| :--------------------- | :--------------------- | :-------------------------------------- |
| English                | 100                    | —                                       |
| General Knowledge      | 100                    | ~8–10 questions (Economics sub-section) |
| Elementary Mathematics | 100 (IMA/INA/AFA only) | —                                       |

GK as a whole spans History, Geography, Polity, Science, Current Affairs, and Economics — roughly 12 sub-sections total. Economics questions draw mostly from **static concepts + terminology** (GDP/GNP/NNP, RBI functions, banking structure) rather than deep theory, plus a current-affairs layer (Budget, schemes, latest RBI policy moves).

> [!note] Key Idea
> Because this sits inside GK, not a dedicated Economics paper, depth expectations are shallower than something like a UPSC Mains GS paper — but breadth expectations are higher. PW's course reflects this: broad chapter coverage, moderate depth per chapter, heavy terminology and PYQ drilling.

## 2. Official CDS Economics Topic List (as tested)

Based on UPSC's actual question patterns and officially notified GK scope:

- National Income concepts — GDP, GNP, NNP, PCI, PDI (basic definitions, differences, formulas)
- Money & Banking — evolution of money, RBI functions, banking structure, NBFCs
- Monetary Policy — RBI policy tools, repo rate, CRR/SLR, MCLR/EBLR
- Fiscal Policy — Union Budget structure, deficit types, taxation (direct/indirect, wealth/income/service tax)
- Five-Year Plans & Planning Commission / NITI Aayog
- Foreign Trade & Forex — BoP, FDI, FERA/FEMA, forex reserves
- International Organizations — IMF, World Bank, WTO, IDA
- Poverty & Employment Schemes — JNNURM, PMGSY, MGNREGA-type programs
- Agriculture Economy — Green/White/Blue Revolution, leading crop/commodity producers
- Inflation & Currency — types of inflation, currency management, demonetization
- Census & Demographics (economics-adjacent GK)
- Industries — major Indian industries, industrial policy basics
