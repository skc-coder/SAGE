# Koeppen Climate Classification and Indian Climate

## Theory, Intuition & Core Concepts

Koeppen Climate Scheme (A, B, C, D, E major groups, s, w, f precipitation sub-types), Tropical Rainforest, Savanna, Steppe, Mediterranean, Tundra climates, Indian Monsoon mechanism, and El Niño / La Niña effects.
