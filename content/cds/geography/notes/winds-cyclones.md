Temperate Cyclones
♤ Develops in temperate region bw 35-65 degree latitude ( Mid & high
latitude ).
♤ Form along polar front, initially it is stationary front.
♤ In NH, warm air moves from south and cold air from north form
front, when the pressure drops along front, warm air moves northward
& cold southward causing anticlockwise circulation.
♤ Cold air lifts the warm air from behind and chases it, forming anti
circulation motion.
♤ These warm air forms cumulus clouds causing rain.
♤ If Occluded front forms, means completely lifted up, cyclone
dissipates.
♤ This moisture in air is also lifted by subtropical jet streams into India
causing western disturbances (from meditarranean sea).
