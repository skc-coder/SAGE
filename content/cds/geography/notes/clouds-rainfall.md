Types of
Condensation
Dew
◇ When the moisture is deposited in form
of water droplets on cooler surface of solid
objects such as stone, grass, blades and
plant leaves.
◇ Do not deposit on nuclei in air above
suface - Surface Phenomena.
◇ Ideal condition :- 1.Clear sky
2.Calm air
3.High relative humidity
4.Cold & long nights.
◇ Dew point should be above freezing point.
Frost
◇ Forms on cold surfaces, when
condensation takes place below freezing
point (0 degree C).
◇ When dew point is at or below the
freezing point. - Surface Phenomena
◇ Excess moisture is deposited in the form
of minute ice crystals.
◇ Ideal condition :- Same as that of Dew
except that the air temperature must be at
or below freezing point.
Fog & Mist
◇ When temperature of an air mass
containing a large quantity of water vapour
falls all of a sudden, condensation takes
place within itself on fine dust particles.
◇ A cloud with its base at or very near the
ground.
◇ Visibility becomes zero.

◇ Drier than mist, prevalent where warm
currents of air come in contact of cold.
◇ Are mini clouds formed by cond.

◇ Radiation Fog - forms when the ground cools
at night, causing the air near the surface to also
cool and reach its dew point, leading to
condensation.
◇Advection fog, on the other hand, occurs when
warm moist air moves over a cooler surface,
causing it to cool and reach its dew point
MIST
◇ Contain more (each nuclei contains
thicker layer of moisture).
◇ Comparitively more visibility.
◇ Frequent over mountains as the
rising warm air up the slopes meets
cold surface.
SMOG
◇ Urban & industrial area produce smoke,
leading more nuclei for fog & mist
formation.
◇ When Fog mixes with this smoke, called
smog.
◇ Formation of inversion lid is must for
Smog - a layer of warm air that traps cooler
air and pollutants close to the surface,
preventing them from dispersing.

**Embedded Text / PYQs:**
Rainfall
◇ In form of water called
rainfall.
Types of Clouds -
Height Basis
Snowfall
◇ When the temperature is
lower than the 0 degree C,
precipitation takes place in
form of snowfall.
◇ Other forms of
precipitation are sleet & hail.
Sleet
◇ Sleet is frozen raindrops and
refrozen melted snow water.
◇ When a layer of air with the
temperature above freezing point
overlies a sub freezing layer near
the ground, precipitation takes
place in form of sleet.
◇ Raindrops, which leave the
warmer air, encounter the colder air
below, and solidifies and reach
ground as small pellets of ice not
bigger than the raindrops from
which they formed.
Hail
◇ Sometime, drops of rain
after being released by the
clouds, become solidified into
small rounded solid pieces of
ice reaching earth's surface
called hailstones.
◇ These are formed by the
rainwater passing through the
colder layers.
◇ Hailstones have several
concentric layers of ice one
over the other.
Graupel
Graupel, also called soft hail or
hominy snow or granular snow
or snow pellets, is precipitation
that forms when supercooled
water droplets in air are
collected and freeze on falling
snowflakes.
