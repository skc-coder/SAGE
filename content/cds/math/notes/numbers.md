# Number System

## Theory, Intuition & Formulas

### Ratio and Proportion Basics
Equal ratios of the form $\frac{a}{x} = \frac{b}{y} = \frac{c}{z} = k$ express direct proportionality among quantities.

Key Theorems & Tricks:
1. **$k$-Method**: Assign a constant $k$ to express variables as multiples ($a=xk, b=yk, c=zk$).
2. **Theorem of Equal Ratios (Addendo)**:
   $$\frac{a}{x} = \frac{b}{y} = \frac{c}{z} = \frac{a + b + c}{x + y + z}$$
3. **Direct Value Substitution**: In homogeneous expressions (where degree of numerator equals degree of denominator), replace $a, b, c$ directly with their proportional values $x, y, z$.
