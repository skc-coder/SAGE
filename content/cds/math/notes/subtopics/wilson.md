# Wilson's Theorem

## Statement

An integer $p > 1$ is prime if and only if:

$$\mathbf{(p - 1)! \equiv -1 \pmod p} \quad \text{or} \quad \mathbf{(p - 1)! + 1 \equiv 0 \pmod p}$$
