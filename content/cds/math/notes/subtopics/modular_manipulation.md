# Modular Fast Power Reduction & Negative Remainders

## Core Practical Manipulation Techniques

### 1. Formal Euclidean Definition of Negative Dividends & Remainders
$A = B \cdot Q + R \implies 0 \le R < B$.

### 2. Negative Remainder Conversion Rule
$-k \pmod m \equiv (-k + m) \pmod m$.

### 3. Modular Inverse & Cancellation
$a \cdot a^{-1} \equiv 1 \pmod m$. Exists if and only if $\operatorname{GCD}(a, m) = 1$.
