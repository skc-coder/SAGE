# CDS Practice Q45: Spherical Balloon Subtended Angle & Elevation

## Problem Statement

A spherical balloon of radius $r$ subtends an angle $60^\circ$ at the eye of an observer. If the angle of elevation of its center is $60^\circ$, what is the height of the center of the balloon above the horizontal plane?

- (a) $r$
- (b) $r\sqrt{3}$
- (c) $\frac{r}{\sqrt{3}}$
- (d) $2r$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Apply Subtended Angle Formula
Refer to [[content/cds/math/notes/subtopics/height_spherical_balloon|Subtended Angles & Spherical Geometry]].

Given parameters:
- Radius of sphere: $r$
- Total subtended angle at observer: $\alpha = 60^\circ \implies \frac{\alpha}{2} = 30^\circ$
- Angle of elevation of center: $\beta = 60^\circ$

### Step 2: Distance from Observer to Center ($OC$)
In right-angled triangle formed by observer $O$, center $C$, and point of tangency $T$:

$$\sin\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}\right) = \sin 30^\circ = \frac{r}{OC}$$

$$\frac{1}{2} = \frac{r}{OC} \implies OC = 2r$$

### Step 3: Vertical Height of Center ($h$)
In right-angled triangle formed by $O$, $C$, and projection of center on ground $H$:

$$\sin \beta = \sin 60^\circ = \frac{h}{OC}$$

$$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{h}{2r} \implies h = 2r \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = r\sqrt{3}$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(b) $r\sqrt{3}$**
