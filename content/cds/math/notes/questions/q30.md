# CDS Practice Q30: Multi-Polynomial Numerical & Variable HCF

## Question Text

What is the HCF of the three polynomials?
$$P(x) = 36(3x^4 + 5x^3 - 2x^2)$$
$$Q(x) = 9(6x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x)$$
$$R(x) = 54(27x^4 - x)$$

**Options:**
- (a) $9x(x + 1)$
- (b) $9x(3x - 1)$
- (c) $18x(3x - 1)$
- (d) $18x(x + 1)$

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Step 1: Compute HCF of Numerical Coefficients
Coefficients are $36$, $9$, and $54$.
$$\operatorname{gcd}(36, 9, 54) = 9 \quad \text{or for } Q(x) = 9(6x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x) = 18(3x^3 + 2x^2 - x)$$
Notice inside $Q(x)$, $2$ can be factored out: $Q(x) = 18x(3x^2 + 2x - 1) = 18x(3x - 1)(x + 1)$.
Let's factor all three polynomials completely:

1. $P(x) = 36x^2(3x^2 + 5x - 2) = 36x^2(3x - 1)(x + 2)$
2. $Q(x) = 9x(6x^2 + 4x - 2) = 18x(3x^2 + 2x - 1) = 18x(3x - 1)(x + 1)$
3. $R(x) = 54x(27x^3 - 1) = 54x(3x - 1)(9x^2 + 3x + 1)$

### Step 2: Extract Common Factors
- Scalar coefficient: $\operatorname{gcd}(36, 18, 54) = 18$.
- Common power of $x$: $\min(x^2, x, x) = x$.
- Common algebraic factor: $(3x - 1)$.

$$\operatorname{HCF}(P, Q, R) = 18x(3x - 1)$$

**Correct Answer**: **(c) $18x(3x - 1)$**

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
Extract scalar factors and variable powers ($x^k$) first! Do not forget to factor out numeric common terms inside parentheses (like $2$ in $6x^2+4x-2$).
