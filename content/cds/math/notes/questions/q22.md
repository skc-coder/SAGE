# Question 22: Circular Track Concurrency Meeting Time

John, Kate, and Smith at the same time, same point, and in the same direction run around a circular track in 250 seconds, 300 seconds, and 150 seconds respectively. Find after what time will they meet again?

(a) 30 min  
(b) 25 min  
(c) 20 min  
(d) 15 min  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Concept: Circular Concurrency = LCM of Lap Times
> They will meet together at the starting point at any time $T$ that is a common multiple of all three individual lap times ($250\text{ s}, 300\text{ s}, 150\text{ s}$).  
> The **first time** they meet again is the **Least Common Multiple (LCM)**.
> 
> ### Calculation:
> 1. Prime factorization of lap times:
>    - $250 = 2 \times 5^3$
>    - $300 = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5^2$
>    - $150 = 2 \times 3 \times 5^2$
> 
> 2. $\operatorname{LCM}(250, 300, 150) = 2^2 \times 3 \times 5^3 = 4 \times 3 \times 125 = \mathbf{1500 \text{ seconds}}$.
> 
> 3. Convert seconds to minutes:
>    $$\text{Time} = \frac{1500}{60} = \mathbf{25 \text{ minutes}}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 25 min**
