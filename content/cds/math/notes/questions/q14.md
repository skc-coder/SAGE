# Question 14: Sum of Powers Modulo Prime (1^12 + 2^12 + ... + 12^12 mod 13)

What is the remainder when $(1^{12} + 2^{12} + 3^{12} + \dots + 12^{12})$ is divided by 13?

(a) 0  
(b) 1  
(c) 12  
(d) 6  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> 1. **Check conditions for Fermat's Little Theorem**:
>    - Modulus $p = 13$ (13 is prime).
>    - For each base $x \in \{1, 2, \dots, 12\}$, $\operatorname{GCD}(x, 13) = 1$.
> 2. **Apply FLT to each term ($x^{13-1} = x^{12} \equiv 1 \pmod{13}$)**:
>    - $1^{12} \equiv 1 \pmod{13}$
>    - $2^{12} \equiv 1 \pmod{13}$
>    - $\dots$
>    - $12^{12} \equiv 1 \pmod{13}$
> 3. **Sum all 12 terms**:
>    $$\text{Sum} \equiv \underbrace{1 + 1 + 1 + \dots + 1}_{12 \text{ terms}} = 12 \pmod{13}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(c) 12 (or -1)**
