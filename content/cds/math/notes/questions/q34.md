# CDS Practice Q34: Polynomial Recovery from Sum & Difference (PYQ 2016 I Q41)

## Question Text

The sum and difference of two algebraic expressions $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ are:
$$P(x) + Q(x) = 5x^2 - x - 4$$
$$P(x) - Q(x) = x^2 + 9x - 10$$

Find the HCF of the two expressions.

**Options:**
- (a) $x + 1$
- (b) $x - 1$
- (c) $3x + 7$
- (d) $2x - 3$

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Method 1: The 3-Second Bézout Linear Combination Shortcut (Genius Exam Hack)

> [!TIP]
> **Bézout's Lemma Shortcut**:
> The HCF of $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ **MUST divide any linear combination of $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$**.
> In particular, $\operatorname{HCF}(P, Q)$ MUST divide BOTH:
> 1. $\text{Sum} = P(x) + Q(x) = 5x^2 - x - 4$
> 2. $\text{Diff} = P(x) - Q(x) = x^2 + 9x - 10$

Therefore, the root of the true HCF option **MUST make BOTH $\text{Sum} = 0$ AND $\text{Diff} = 0$**!

#### Test Option (b) $x - 1 \implies x = 1$:
- Plug $x = 1$ into $\text{Sum}$:
  $$\text{Sum}(1) = 5(1)^2 - (1) - 4 = 5 - 1 - 4 = 0 \quad \checkmark$$
- Plug $x = 1$ into $\text{Diff}$:
  $$\text{Diff}(1) = (1)^2 + 9(1) - 10 = 1 + 9 - 10 = 0 \quad \checkmark$$

Since $x = 1$ makes **both $\text{Sum} = 0$ AND $\text{Diff} = 0$**, **Option (b) $(x - 1)$ is the HCF instantly without finding $P(x)$ or $Q(x)$!**

---

### Method 2: Standard Recovery & Factorization (Long Way)

Add both given equations to find $P(x)$:
$$2 P(x) = (5x^2 - x - 4) + (x^2 + 9x - 10) = 6x^2 + 8x - 14$$
$$P(x) = 3x^2 + 4x - 7 = (x - 1)(3x + 7)$$

Subtract equations to find $Q(x)$:
$$2 Q(x) = (5x^2 - x - 4) - (x^2 + 9x - 10) = 4x^2 - 10x + 6$$
$$Q(x) = 2x^2 - 5x + 3 = (x - 1)(2x - 3)$$

$$\operatorname{HCF}(P(x), Q(x)) = (x - 1)$$

**Correct Answer**: **(b) $(x - 1)$**

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
> [!NOTE]
> **Zero-Work Exam Rule**:
> Never waste time solving $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ when Sum and Diff are given! Just plug option roots directly into $\text{Sum}$ and $\text{Diff}$. The option that zeroes BOTH is your HCF.
