# CDS Practice Q34: Polynomial Recovery from Sum & Difference

## Question Text

The sum and difference of two algebraic expressions $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ are:
$$P(x) + Q(x) = 5x^2 - x - 4$$
$$P(x) - Q(x) = x^2 + 9x - 10$$

Find:
1. The LCM of $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$.
2. The HCF of $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$.

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Step 1: Recover Individual Polynomials $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$

Add both given equations:
$$2 P(x) = (5x^2 - x - 4) + (x^2 + 9x - 10)$$
$$2 P(x) = 6x^2 + 8x - 14$$
$$P(x) = 3x^2 + 4x - 7$$

Subtract the second equation from the first equation:
$$2 Q(x) = (5x^2 - x - 4) - (x^2 + 9x - 10)$$
$$2 Q(x) = 4x^2 - 10x + 6$$
$$Q(x) = 2x^2 - 5x + 3$$

---

### Step 2: Factorize Both Expressions

#### Factorizing $P(x)$:
Split middle term $4x = 7x - 3x$:
$$P(x) = 3x^2 + 7x - 3x - 7$$
$$P(x) = x(3x + 7) - 1(3x + 7)$$
$$P(x) = (x - 1)(3x + 7)$$

#### Factorizing $Q(x)$:
Split middle term $-5x = -2x - 3x$:
$$Q(x) = 2x^2 - 2x - 3x + 3$$
$$Q(x) = 2x(x - 1) - 3(x - 1)$$
$$Q(x) = (x - 1)(2x - 3)$$

---

### Step 3: Compute HCF & LCM

- Common linear factor:
  $$\operatorname{HCF}(P(x), Q(x)) = (x - 1)$$

- Product of all distinct linear factors:
  $$\operatorname{LCM}(P(x), Q(x)) = (x - 1)(2x - 3)(3x + 7)$$

**Results**:
- **HCF**: $(x - 1)$
- **LCM**: $(x - 1)(2x - 3)(3x + 7)$

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
When sum $S(x)$ and difference $D(x)$ are given:
$$P(x) = \frac{S(x) + D(x)}{2}$$
$$Q(x) = \frac{S(x) - D(x)}{2}$$
Factorize the isolated expressions line-by-line to obtain both HCF and LCM seamlessly.
