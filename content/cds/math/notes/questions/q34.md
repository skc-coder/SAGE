# CDS Practice Q34: Polynomial Recovery from Sum & Difference

## Question Text

The sum and difference of two algebraic expressions $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ are:
$$P(x) + Q(x) = 5x^2 - x - 4$$
$$P(x) - Q(x) = x^2 + 9x - 10$$

Find:
1. The LCM of $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$.
2. The HCF of $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$.

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Step 1: Recover Individual Polynomials $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$
Add both equations:
$$2 P(x) = (5x^2 - x - 4) + (x^2 + 9x - 10) = 6x^2 + 8x - 14$$
$$P(x) = 3x^2 + 4x - 7$$

Subtract second equation from first:
$$2 Q(x) = (5x^2 - x - 4) - (x^2 + 9x - 10) = 4x^2 - 10x + 6$$
$$Q(x) = 2x^2 - 5x + 3$$

### Step 2: Factorize Both Expressions
1. $P(x) = 3x^2 + 7x - 3x - 7 = x(3x + 7) - 1(3x + 7) = (x - 1)(3x + 7)$
2. $Q(x) = 2x^2 - 2x - 3x + 3 = 2x(x - 1) - 3(x - 1) = (x - 1)(2x - 3)$

### Step 3: Compute HCF & LCM
- $\operatorname{HCF}(P(x), Q(x)) = (x - 1)$
- $\operatorname{LCM}(P(x), Q(x)) = (x - 1)(2x - 3)(3x + 7)$

**Results**:
- **HCF**: $(x - 1)$
- **LCM**: $(x - 1)(2x - 3)(3x + 7)$

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
When sum $S(x)$ and difference $D(x)$ are given:
$$P(x) = \frac{S(x) + D(x)}{2}, \quad Q(x) = \frac{S(x) - D(x)}{2}$$
Factorize the isolated expressions directly to obtain both HCF and LCM seamlessly.
