# CDS Practice Q47: Cloud and Water Reflection Depression Model

## Problem Statement

Let $P$ be a cloud at height $H\text{ m}$ above the surface of a lake. An observer at point $B$ at height $200\text{ m}$ above the lake observes the angle of elevation of the cloud $P$ to be $30^\circ$ and the angle of depression of its image $Q$ in the lake to be $60^\circ$. What is the height $H$ of the cloud above the lake?

- (a) $300\text{ m}$
- (b) $350\text{ m}$
- (c) $400\text{ m}$
- (d) $450\text{ m}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Establish Reflection Principles
- Water level is at height $0$.
- Observer $B$ is at height $h = 200\text{ m}$.
- Cloud $P$ is at height $H$ above water $\implies$ height of cloud above observer level is $(H - 200)\text{ m}$.
- Image $Q$ is at distance $H$ below water level $\implies$ depth of image below observer level is $(H + 200)\text{ m}$.

### Step 2: Formulate Elevation and Depression Tangents
Let $x$ be the horizontal distance from observer $B$ to the vertical line of the cloud.

1. In right-angled triangle for cloud $P$ ($\text{elevation } 30^\circ$):

$$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{H - 200}{x} \implies x = \sqrt{3}(H - 200)$$

2. In right-angled triangle for image $Q$ ($\text{depression } 60^\circ$):

$$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{H + 200}{x} \implies x = \frac{H + 200}{\sqrt{3}}$$

### Step 3: Equate Horizontal Separation $x$

$$\sqrt{3}(H - 200) = \frac{H + 200}{\sqrt{3}}$$

$$3(H - 200) = H + 200$$

$$3H - 600 = H + 200$$

$$2H = 800 \implies H = 400\text{ m}$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(c) $400\text{ m}$**
