# CDS Practice Q44: Speed of Boat from Tower Depression

## Problem Statement

A man is watching from the top of a tower a boat speeding away from the tower. The boat makes an angle of depression of $45^\circ$ with the man's eye when at a distance of $60\text{ m}$ from the bottom of tower. After $5\text{ s}$, the angle of depression becomes $30^\circ$. What is the approximate speed of the boat assuming that it is running in still water?

- (a) $31.5\text{ km/h}$
- (b) $36.5\text{ km/h}$
- (c) $38.5\text{ km/h}$
- (d) $40.5\text{ km/h}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Find Tower Height ($h$)
Let $AB = h$ be the height of the tower, and $C$ be the initial position of the boat at distance $BC = 60\text{ m}$.
Since the initial angle of depression is $45^\circ$, the angle of elevation $\angle ACB = 45^\circ$.

In right-angled $\Delta ABC$:

$$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{h}{60} \implies h = 60\text{ m}$$

### Step 2: Find Final Position Distance ($BD$)
Let $D$ be the position of the boat after $5\text{ s}$ when the angle of depression becomes $30^\circ$ ($\angle ADB = 30^\circ$).

In right-angled $\Delta ABD$:

$$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{AB}{BD} = \frac{60}{BD}$$

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{60}{BD} \implies BD = 60\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$

### Step 3: Compute Distance Traveled ($CD$)
The distance covered by the boat in $5\text{ s}$ is:

$$CD = BD - BC = 60\sqrt{3} - 60 = 60(\sqrt{3} - 1)\text{ m}$$

Substitute $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$:

$$CD = 60(1.732 - 1) = 60 \times 0.732 = 43.92\text{ m}$$

### Step 4: Convert Speed to $\text{km/h}$
- Speed in $\text{m/s}$:

$$v = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{43.92\text{ m}}{5\text{ s}} = 8.784\text{ m/s}$$

- Convert to $\text{km/h}$ by multiplying by $\frac{18}{5} = 3.6$:

$$v = 8.784 \times 3.6 = 31.6224\text{ km/h} \approx 31.5\text{ km/h}$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(a) $31.5\text{ km/h}$**
