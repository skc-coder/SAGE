# Q37

## Question Statement

What is the total number of three digit numbers with unit digit 7 and divisible by 11?

(a) 6  
(b) 7  
(c) 8  
(d) 9  

## Solution

### Method 1: AP & Units Place Analysis (Fastest ⚡)

1. **Find Smallest 3-Digit Number Ending in 7 Divisible by 11**:
   - Numbers ending in 7 divisible by 11 are of the form $11 \times k$, where the units digit of $k$ must be **7** (since $1 \times 7 = 7$).
   - Smallest positive integer $k$ ending in 7: $k = 7 \implies 11 \times 7 = 77$ (2-digit, invalid).
   - Next integer $k$ ending in 7: $k = 17 \implies 11 \times 17 = 187$ (3-digit, valid!).
   - First term $a = 187$.

2. **Common Difference $d$**:
   - Since $k$ increases by $10$ each time ($17, 27, 37, \dots$), the numbers increase by:
     $$d = 11 \times 10 = 110$$

3. **Find Largest 3-Digit Term ($l < 1000$)**:
   - Sequence: $187, 297, 407, 517, 627, 737, 847, 957$.
   - Last term $l = 957$.

4. **Calculate Total Count ($n$)**:
   - $l = a + (n - 1)d$
   - $957 = 187 + (n - 1)110 \implies 770 = (n - 1)110 \implies n - 1 = 7 \implies n = 8$.

**Correct Answer:** **(c) 8**
