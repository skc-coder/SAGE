# Question 17: Greatest Number Leaving Different Remainders (CDS 2014 I)

Find the greatest number which will divide $400$, $435$, and $541$ leaving $9$, $10$, and $14$ as remainders respectively.

(a) 19  
(b) 17  
(c) 13  
(d) 9  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Concept: HCF of Differences
> Let the required divisor be $D$.
> - $400 \div D$ leaves remainder $9 \implies (400 - 9) = 391$ is exactly divisible by $D$.
> - $435 \div D$ leaves remainder $10 \implies (435 - 10) = 425$ is exactly divisible by $D$.
> - $541 \div D$ leaves remainder $14 \implies (541 - 14) = 527$ is exactly divisible by $D$.
> 
> Therefore, $D = \operatorname{HCF}(391, 425, 527)$.
> 
> ### Calculation using Difference Trick:
> 1. Find difference between closest numbers:  
>    $425 - 391 = 34$.
> 2. The HCF must be a factor of 34 ($34 = 2 \times 17$).
> 3. Since 391, 425, 527 are all odd, 2 is ruled out.
> 4. Test 17:
>    - $391 = 17 \times 23$
>    - $425 = 17 \times 25$
>    - $527 = 17 \times 31$
> 
> $\implies D = \mathbf{17}$.
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 17**
