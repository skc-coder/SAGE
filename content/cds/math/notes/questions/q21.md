# Question 21: Measuring Vessel Maximum Capacity (Drums Problem)

There are three drums with 1653 litre, 2261 litre, and 2527 litre of petrol. The greatest possible size of the measuring vessel with which we can measure the petrol of any drum while every time the vessel must be completely filled is:

(a) 31  
(b) 27  
(c) 19  
(d) 41  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Concept: Maximum Capacity = HCF of Capacities
> The vessel must divide 1653, 2261, and 2527 without leaving any fractional remainder.
> Therefore, Capacity = $\operatorname{HCF}(1653, 2261, 2527)$.
> 
> ### Difference Property:
> 1. Difference between $2527 - 2261 = \mathbf{266}$.
> 2. Factor 266: $266 = 14 \times 19 = 2 \times 7 \times 19$.
> 3. Since all drum capacities are odd, 2 and 14 are ruled out.
> 4. Test option 19:
>    - $1653 = 19 \times 87$
>    - $2261 = 19 \times 119$
>    - $2527 = 19 \times 133$
> 
> $\implies \text{Vessel Size} = \mathbf{19 \text{ litres}}$.
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(c) 19**
