# Q26

## Question Statement

What is the sum of positive integers less than 100 which leave a remainder 1 when divided by 3 and leave a remainder 2 when divided by 4?

(a) 416  
(b) 620  
(c) 1250  
(d) 1314  

## Solution

### Method 1: AP Formula & Chinese Remainder Principle (Fastest ⚡)

1. **Find General Form of Number $N$**:
   - $N \equiv 1 \pmod 3$
   - $N \equiv 2 \pmod 4$

   Smallest positive integer satisfying both conditions:
   - Numbers $\equiv 2 \pmod 4$: $2, 6, 10, 14, \dots$
   - Check mod 3: $2 \equiv 2$, $6 \equiv 0$, $10 \equiv 1 \pmod 3$.
   - First term $a = 10$.

2. **Common Difference $d$**:
   $$d = \operatorname{LCM}(3, 4) = 12$$

   So $N$ forms an Arithmetic Progression: $10, 22, 34, 46, 58, 70, 82, 94$.

3. **Number of Terms $n$**:
   $$a_n = a + (n-1)d < 100 \implies 10 + (n-1)12 \le 94 \implies 12(n-1) = 84 \implies n = 8$$

4. **Sum of AP**:
   $$S_n = \frac{n}{2} (a + l) = \frac{8}{2} (10 + 94) = 4 \times 104 = 416$$

**Correct Answer:** **(a) 416**
