# Question 26: Sum of Digits of Least Multiple with Constant Difference (CDS 2015 II)

What is the sum of digits of the least multiple of 13, which when divided by 6, 8, and 12 leaves 5, 7, and 11, respectively, as the remainders?

(a) 5  
(b) 6  
(c) 7  
(d) 8  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Step 1: Check Constant Difference $p$
> - $6 - 5 = 1$
> - $8 - 7 = 1$
> - $12 - 11 = 1$
> 
> Constant difference $p = 1$.
> 
> ### Step 2: Form general general expression
> $$\operatorname{LCM}(6, 8, 12) = 24$$
> $$N = 24k - 1$$
> 
> ### Step 3: Apply divisibility condition ($N \equiv 0 \pmod{13}$)
> $$24k - 1 \equiv 0 \pmod{13}$$
> $$11k - 1 \equiv 0 \pmod{13} \implies 11k \equiv 1 \pmod{13}$$
> 
> Test integer values of $k$:
> - For $k = 6 \implies 11(6) = 66 = 5(13) + 1 \equiv 1 \pmod{13}$ ✅.
> 
> ### Step 4: Calculate $N$ and sum of digits
> $$N = 24(6) - 1 = 144 - 1 = \mathbf{143}$$
> 
> Verify: $143 = 13 \times 11$ (divisible by 13!).
> $$\text{Sum of digits} = 1 + 4 + 3 = \mathbf{8}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(d) 8**
