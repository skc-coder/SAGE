# CDS Practice Q31: Euclidean Long Division for High-Degree Polynomials

## Question Text

Find the HCF of the polynomials:
$$P(x) = 22x^6 - 78x^5 - 16x^2$$
$$Q(x) = 2x^5 - 78x^4 - 44x^2$$

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Step 1: Extract Common Monomials
- $P(x) = 2x^2(11x^4 - 39x^3 - 8)$
- $Q(x) = 2x^2(x^3 - 39x - 22)$

Common monomial HCF is $2x^2$.
Now find HCF of inner polynomials:
$$f(x) = 11x^4 - 39x^3 - 8$$
$$g(x) = x^4 - 39x - 22$$

### Step 2: Perform Long Division $f(x) \div g(x)$
Dividing $11x^4 - 39x^3 - 8$ by $x^4 - 39x - 22$:
Quotient $= 11$.
$$11 \cdot (x^4 - 39x - 22) = 11x^4 - 429x - 242$$
Subtracting:
$$\text{Remainder} = -39x^3 + 429x + 234$$

### Step 3: Extract Scalar Common Factor from Remainder
Extract $-39$:
$$-39(x^3 - 11x - 6)$$

### Step 4: Next Division Step
Divide $g(x) = x^4 - 39x - 22$ by new divisor $x^3 - 11x - 6$:
$$x^4 - 39x - 22 = x(x^3 - 11x - 6) + (11x^2 - 33x - 22)$$
Extract scalar $11$ from remainder:
$$11(x^2 - 3x - 2)$$

### Step 5: Final Division Step
Divide $x^3 - 11x - 6$ by $x^2 - 3x - 2$:
$$x^3 - 11x - 6 = (x + 3)(x^2 - 3x - 2) + 0$$
Remainder is $0$.

Thus the polynomial HCF is $x^2 - 3x - 2$.

Combining with monomial HCF $2x^2$:
$$\operatorname{HCF}(P(x), Q(x)) = 2x^2(x^2 - 3x - 2)$$

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
When degree is high and factoring is non-obvious, extract $x^k$ first, then run Euclidean long division. Always remove scalar factors from remainders at every step!
