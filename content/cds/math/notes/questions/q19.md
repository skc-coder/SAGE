# Question 19: Product Identity with Linear LCM-HCF Sum (CDS 2014 II)

The LCM of two numbers is 90 times their HCF. The sum of LCM and HCF is 1456. If one of the numbers is 160, then what is the other number?

(a) 120  
(b) 136  
(c) 144  
(d) 184  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Step 1: Find HCF ($H$) and LCM ($L$)
> Let $\operatorname{HCF} = x$.  
> Given $\operatorname{LCM} = 90x$.
> 
> $$\operatorname{LCM} + \operatorname{HCF} = 1456 \implies 90x + x = 1456 \implies 91x = 1456$$
> $$x = \frac{1456}{91} = \mathbf{16}$$
> 
> Thus:
> - $\operatorname{HCF} = 16$
> - $\operatorname{LCM} = 90 \times 16 = 1440$
> 
> ### Step 2: Apply Fundamental Product Identity
> $$\operatorname{HCF} \times \operatorname{LCM} = A \times B$$
> $$16 \times 1440 = 160 \times B$$
> $$B = \frac{16 \times 1440}{160} = \frac{1440}{10} = \mathbf{144}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(c) 144**
