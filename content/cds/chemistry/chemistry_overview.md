# Chemistry Subject Overview

Welcome to the **Chemistry Master Study Vault** for the Combined Defence Services (CDS) & Defense Exams. Below is the structured curriculum, chapter breakdown, and quick reference guide.

---

## Chapter Breakdown & Vault Notes

- [[cds/chemistry/notes/classification_of_matter|Ch 01. Classification of Matter & Physical States]]
  - 4th & 5th States of Matter (Plasma & Bose-Einstein Condensate)
  - Latent Heat, Gas Laws (Boyle, Charles, Gay-Lussac, Avogadro)
  - True Solutions, Colloids (Aerosol, Emulsion, Sol, Gel, Foam), Suspensions
  - Physical vs. Chemical Changes, Separation Techniques (Distillation, Sublimation, Centrifugation)

- [[cds/chemistry/notes/atoms_molecules|Ch 02. Atoms, Molecules & Atomic Structure]]
  - Laws of Chemical Combination (Mass Conservation, Definite & Multiple Proportions)
  - Dalton's Atomic Theory, Berzelius Symbol System
  - Valency Rules (The Magic 8 Rule, Octet Logic)
  - Subatomic Particles ($e^-, p^+, n^0$), Atomic Models (Rutherford, Bohr)
  - Isotopes, Isobars, Isotones, Isoelectronic species

- [[cds/chemistry/notes/chemical_reactions|Ch 03. Chemical Reactions & Equations]]
  - Types of Reactions: Combination, Decomposition, Displacement, Double Displacement
  - Exothermic vs. Endothermic processes
  - Redox Reactions: Oxidation (loss of $e^-$ / gain of $O$) & Reduction (gain of $e^-$ / loss of $O$)
  - Corrosion of Metals (Iron Rusting, Silver Tarnishing, Green Copper Carbonate)
  - Rancidity & Prevention ($N_2$ gas packaging)

- [[cds/chemistry/notes/acids_bases_salts|Ch 04. Acids, Bases, Salts & Indicators]]
  - Theories: Arrhenius, Brønsted-Lowry (Proton transfer), Lewis (Electron-pair donor/acceptor)
  - Indicators: Litmus (Lichen), Turmeric, Red Cabbage, Phenolphthalein, Methyl Orange, Olfactory
  - pH Scale ($0-14$), Standard pH values (Gastric juice, Blood, Milk of Magnesia, Lemon)
  - Natural Acids (Acetic, Citric, Tartaric, Oxalic, Lactic, Methanoic/Formic)
  - Industrial & Chemical Salts: Chlor-Alkali Process ($NaOH$), Bleaching Powder ($\text{CaOCl}_2$), Baking Soda ($\text{NaHCO}_3$), Washing Soda ($\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3 \cdot 10\text{H}_2\text{O}$), Plaster of Paris ($\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$)
  - Water of Crystallization (Vitriols: Green, Blue, White; Epsom Salt, Gypsum)

- [[cds/chemistry/notes/common_names|Ch 05. Common Names & Chemical Formulas]]
  - Master Table of Trivial Names, IUPAC Names, and Chemical Formulas
  - Industrial Manufacturing Processes (Haber, Contact, Ostwald, Solvay, Chlor-Alkali)
  - Sublimable Solids, Ores & Alloy Compositions
