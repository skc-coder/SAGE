# Trigonometry Chapter Variations

Here are novel multi-concept Tier 2 & Tier 3 chapter variations for CDS Elementary Mathematics.

## Tier 2 Topic Variation: Heights & Distances with Moving Objects

### Problem 1: Constant Velocity Car towards Tower
A tower of height $120\text{ m}$ stands vertically. From the top of the tower, the angle of depression of a car moving towards the tower at a uniform speed changes from $30^\circ$ to $60^\circ$ in $12\text{ seconds}$. Find the speed of the car in $\text{m/s}$.

> [!faq]- View Solution
> Initial distance $d_1 = \frac{120}{\tan(30^\circ)} = 120\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$.
> Final distance $d_2 = \frac{120}{\tan(60^\circ)} = \frac{120}{\sqrt{3}} = 40\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$.
> Distance covered in $12\text{ seconds} = d_1 - d_2 = 80\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$.
> Speed = $\frac{80\sqrt{3}}{12} = \frac{20\sqrt{3}}{3}\text{ m/s} \approx 11.55\text{ m/s}$.

## Tier 3 Chapter Variation: Combined Trigonometry & Geometry

### Problem 2: Circumcircle & Height Elevation
A vertical pole stands at the center of a circular park of radius $R = 50\text{ m}$. From any point on the boundary of the park, the angle of elevation of the top of the pole is $30^\circ$. If an observer steps back along a radius by a distance $x$, the angle of elevation becomes $15^\circ$. Calculate $x$.

> [!faq]- View Solution
> Height of pole $h = R \tan(30^\circ) = \frac{50}{\sqrt{3}}\text{ m}$.
> New distance from center = $50 + x$.
> $\tan(15^\circ) = 2 - \sqrt{3} = \frac{h}{50+x} = \frac{50/\sqrt{3}}{50+x}$.
> $(50+x)(2-\sqrt{3}) = \frac{50}{\sqrt{3}}$.
> $50+x = \frac{50}{\sqrt{3}(2-\sqrt{3})} = \frac{50(2+\sqrt{3})}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{100}{\sqrt{3}} + 50$.
> $x = \frac{100}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{100\sqrt{3}}{3}\text{ m} \approx 57.73\text{ m}$.
