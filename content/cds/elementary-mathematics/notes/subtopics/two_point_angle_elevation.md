# Two-Point Angle of Elevation

## Theory & Formulas
Questions involving two observations of an object from different distances along a straight line:
$$\tan(\theta_1) = \frac{h}{d_1}, \quad \tan(\theta_2) = \frac{h}{d_2}$$
$$\text{Distance between observers } \Delta d = h(\cot(\theta_1) - \cot(\theta_2))$$
