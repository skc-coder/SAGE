# Ratios and Proportions

## Theory & Properties

Properties of continuous ratios $\frac{a}{p} = \frac{b}{q} = \frac{c}{r}$:
- Homogeneous algebraic fractions can be solved instantly via direct coefficient replacement.
- **Addendo Property**: $\frac{a+b+c}{p+q+r} = \frac{a}{p} = \frac{b}{q} = \frac{c}{r}$.
