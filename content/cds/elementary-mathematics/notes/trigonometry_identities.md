# Heights and Distances

## Core Formulas & Identities

1. **Angle of Elevation & Depression**:
   $$\tan(\theta) = \frac{\text{Perpendicular}}{\text{Base}} = \frac{h}{d}$$

2. **Standard Values**:
   - $\tan(30^\circ) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
   - $\tan(45^\circ) = 1$
   - $\tan(60^\circ) = \sqrt{3}$
   - $\tan(15^\circ) = 2 - \sqrt{3}$
