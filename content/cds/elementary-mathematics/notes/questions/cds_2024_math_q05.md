# CDS 2024 Q05

> **Question Statement**:
> Two circles of radii $9\text{ cm}$ and $4\text{ cm}$ have their centers $13\text{ cm}$ apart. Find the length of the direct common tangent.

> [!faq]- View Solution & Derivation
> Length of direct common tangent $D = \sqrt{d^2 - (R - r)^2}$
> Given $d = 13$, $R = 9$, $r = 4$.
> $D = \sqrt{13^2 - (9 - 4)^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = 12\text{ cm}$.

---

## Tier 1 Direct Question Variation

### Variation 5.1 (Transverse Common Tangent)
For the same two circles, find the length of the transverse common tangent.

> [!faq]- View Solution
> Length of transverse common tangent $T = \sqrt{d^2 - (R + r)^2}$
> $T = \sqrt{13^2 - (9 + 4)^2} = \sqrt{169 - 169} = 0\text{ cm}$ (Circles touch externally).
