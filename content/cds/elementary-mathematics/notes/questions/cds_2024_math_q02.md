# CDS 2024 Q02

> **Question Statement**:
> In an acute-angled triangle $\Delta ABC$, the distance from the orthocenter $H$ to the vertex $A$ is $8\text{ cm}$. If the radius of the circumcircle $R$ is $5\text{ cm}$, find the distance of the orthocenter from the side $BC$.

> [!faq]- View Solution & Derivation
> The distance of the orthocenter $H$ from a side $BC$ is given by $2R \cos(A)$ or $R \cos(A)$ depending on distance formula.
> Distance from vertex $A$ to $H$ is $AH = 2R \cos(A) = 8\text{ cm}$.
> Therefore $\cos(A) = \frac{8}{2(5)} = \frac{4}{5}$.
> Distance of orthocenter $H$ from side $BC$ is $AH' = R \cos(A) = \frac{8}{2} = 4\text{ cm}$ (or $2R \cos(B)\cos(C)$).
> Hence, distance $= 4\text{ cm}$.

## Tier 1 Direct Question Variation

### Variation 2.1 (Euler Line Ratio)
In $\Delta ABC$, the centroid $G$, orthocenter $H$, and circumcenter $O$ lie on the Euler Line. If $HO = 12\text{ cm}$, what is the length of $HG$?

> [!faq]- View Solution
> The centroid $G$ divides the line segment joining the orthocenter $H$ and circumcenter $O$ in the ratio $HG : GO = 2 : 1$.
> Therefore $HG = \frac{2}{3} \times HO = \frac{2}{3} \times 12 = 8\text{ cm}$.
