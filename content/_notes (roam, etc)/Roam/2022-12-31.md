  * Perfect is the enemy of good. The latter is possible while the former is impossible. Don't chase perfectionism. In the case of the study don't try to understand everything in a chapter perfectly. It is not possible and it wastes your time and strains your brain. As a student, your Karma is not to understand everything perfectly but to finish the syllabus in the appropriate time with *enough* level of understanding, the holes in understanding will fill, if we continue to study, later. For eg it was not possible for me to understand well the purpose of odd/even functions in the 12th class but now that I am in the 1st sem, I can see their value. Be patient. #Perfectionsim #Study
