    * Complement method of subtraction:
      * Normal i.e. borrow method is not as efficient as the complement methods. Both for computers and us.
      * Two methods: radix's complement, diminished method
        * See the steps in book.
        * Radix complement:
          * Depending upon if carry is generated (for $$M>= N$$) or not ($$M<N$$) when adding $$M + N^c$$, we discard the carry or take the complement of the sum.
          * But actually, both of these can be done whether carry is generated or not.
          * It is just that it may not be efficient to do so.
        * Diminished radix complement method
          * In this case when carry is gernated (for $$M > N$$) we do *end carry around*. In it we discard the genrate carry and then add 1.
          * In the other case ($$M<= N$$) we take diminished complement of the sum and put minus sign in front.
          * Again both steps ccan be done in either case, it is just the matter of efficiency.
