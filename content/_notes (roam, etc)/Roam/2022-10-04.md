    * On its disadvantages
      * This is futile. 
        * How? Because the *general *textbooks are not meant for such topics, they are not meant to provide deep understanding, and hence you wont get answer from such books and trying to find answer by yourself is shooting at stars. 
        * Worst yet that you wont be able to understand the books that do discus those topics because they require the knowledge from the books which you overlooked for "true/deep understanding".

      * Don't try *too hard *on getting deep understanding now, with such books. Wait for the appropriate time. And for it to indeed come you will need to know the other "things" mentioned in the books. Focus more on them.
