## 5-Month Subject Plan
**Philosophy:** Lectures only for weak/forgotten topics. Every subject = practice + Obsidian notes. Mocks + heavy revision from Month 6 onward.

### Month 1 (Jun 24 – Jul 23): Math + DSA
- Discrete Math: logic, sets, graphs, combinatorics (done ✅), groups/monoids
- Engineering Math: linear algebra, calculus, probability (done  ✅) & statistics (in progress)
- Data Structures: arrays, stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, BSTs, heaps, graphs
- GA: 15–20 min background practice every few days (runs throughout all months)

### Month 2 (Jul 24 – Aug 23): Algorithms + Digital Logic + CO&Arch
- Algorithms: searching, sorting, hashing, complexity, greedy, DP, divide & conquer, graph algos, MST, shortest paths ⚠️ weakest subject (42.9%)
- Digital Logic: Boolean algebra, combinational/sequential circuits, minimization, number representations
- CO&Arch: machine instructions, ALU, pipelining, hazards, memory hierarchy, cache, I/O

### Month 3 (Aug 24 – Sep 23): OS + DBMS
- OS: processes, threads, IPC, synchronization, deadlock, scheduling, memory management, virtual memory, file systems ⚠️ weak (50%)
- DBMS: ER model, relational algebra, SQL, normal forms, indexing (B/B+ trees), transactions, concurrency
- ⚠️ CDS exam Sep 13 — take 2–3 days max that week, no separate deep prep needed

### Month 4 (Sep 24 – Oct 24): Computer Networks + C Programming
- Networks: OSI/TCP-IP, switching, data link, MAC, routing, IP/CIDR/NAT/ARP/DHCP, TCP/UDP, DNS/HTTP/FTP/SMTP
- C Programming: recursion, pointers, arrays — fast pass, piggybacks on DSA

### Month 5 (Oct 25 – Nov 24): ToC + Compiler + Buffer
- ToC: regular expressions, finite automata, CFGs, PDAs, pumping lemma, Turing machines, undecidability ⚠️ weak (50%)
- Compiler Design: lexical analysis, parsing, syntax-directed translation, intermediate code, data flow analysis — fast pass (2–3 marks)
- Buffer: patch any weak subject flagged by mock analysis

### Month 6+ (Nov 25 – Feb 2027): Mocks + Revision
- 1–2 full mocks per week (3 hours each) + next-day review
- Targeted revision on weakest topics from mock analysis
- Final 4 weeks (Jan–Feb): revision sprint only, no new material
- Last 2 weeks: confidence building, light review, routine locked
