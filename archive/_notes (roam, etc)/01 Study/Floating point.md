When we increase the bits for exponents the density is affected. Due to increase in density (provided the total number of bits is same (sign+mantisaa+expo)), the precision is decreased.
Precision here is meant for the ability to represent a given number very closely. As density increases this ability reduces. Here by precision we do not mean the number of digits after point, actually it increases here.

Floating point representation uses the idea of scientific notation, which is used to represent large or small numbers in sundar, and sanchipt way.
