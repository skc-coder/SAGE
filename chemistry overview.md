The notes summarize the **4th and 5th states of matter**:

  

- **Plasma (4th State):** Super-energetic, ionized gas found in glowing fluorescent/neon lights (via electric discharge) and the Sun/stars (driven by high temperatures and nuclear fusion).
    
      
    
- **Bose-Einstein Condensate (5th State):** Formed by super-cooling ultra-low-density gas (about 1/100,000th the density of air) near absolute zero; predicted by S.N. Bose and Einstein, and experimentally realized by Cornell, Wieman, and Ketterle (Nobel Prize 2001).


* **Mechanism:** Increasing heat raises kinetic energy, breaking intermolecular attractions to trigger state changes.
* **Conversion:** $\text{K} = ^\circ\text{C} + 273.15$ (Absolute Zero = $0\text{ K}$).
* **Melting Point (Solid $\rightarrow$ Liquid):** Temperature stays constant during conversion ($0^\circ\text{C}$ for ice). Requires **Latent Heat of Fusion** ($L = Q/M \approx 334\text{ kJ/kg}$ or $80\text{ cal/g}$).
* **Boiling Point (Liquid $\rightarrow$ Gas):** A bulk phenomenon occurring at a constant temperature ($100^\circ\text{C}$ for water). Requires **Latent Heat of Vaporisation** ($\approx 2260\text{ kJ/kg}$ or $540\text{ cal/g}$).


- **Sublimation Origin:** Latin _sublimis_ ("elevated/lifted high"). Alchemists coined it because heating solids turned them directly into vapor that "rose up" the flask without melting into liquid first; chemistry kept the term for solid $\rightarrow$ gas transitions.
    
      
    
- **Vapour Pressure Name:** It is called _vapour_ (not liquid) pressure because it is literally the mechanical outward push exerted by trapped **gas molecules** colliding inside a bubble or sealed chamber.
    
      
    
- **Why We Ignore Water Depth ($\rho gh$):** In a typical pot, water's weight adds less than $1\%$ of the total crushing force compared to atmospheric pressure ($P_{\text{atm}} > 99\%$), and thermodynamic boiling point is formally defined at the zero-depth liquid surface ($h = 0$).

**Question 1**

  

- **Text:** Which one of the following can extinguish fire more quickly?
    
      
    
    _(a) Cold water | (b) Boiling water | (c) Hot water | (d) Ice_
    
      
    
- **TL;DR:** **Boiling water** extinguishes fire fastest because it instantly turns to steam, absorbing massive heat ($2260 \text{ kJ/kg}$) immediately and expanding $1,700\times$ in volume to smother oxygen.
    
      
    

**Question 2**

  

- **Text:** For an ideal gas, which one of the following statements does **not** hold true?
    
      
    
    _(a) The speed of all gas molecules is same. | (b) The kinetic energies of all gas molecules are not same. | (c) The potential energy of the gas molecules is zero. | (d) There is no interactive force between the molecules._
    
      
    
- **TL;DR:** **(a)** is false. Random collisions create a Maxwell-Boltzmann speed distribution across molecules rather than a single uniform speed.
