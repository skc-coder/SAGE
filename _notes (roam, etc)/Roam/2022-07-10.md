          * By making the thing slightly harder but doable. We will get fun and thrill. 
          * The above principle can be applied in life by making everything slightly harder after some time. 
            * One way to do this is by giving less time to do the task that you would normally take.

        * [[Parkinson's Law]]
          * Ask yourself how would you complete a task in half of the time it would be required by the task by you, normally. Ask what if you had day or hour.
          * When you do this you focus on the essentials, the productive things. You cut the useless things.
          * For eg if you had done with the study of, umm, magnetic matter chapter then you wouldn't have given much attention to Curies law or the useless proof of the useless equations which you in fact didn't understand.
